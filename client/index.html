How KindCycle Works

01

Create an Account

Sign up as a Giver or Receiver. Verify your identity for trust and safety.

02

Post or Browse

Givers list items. Receivers browse by category or location proximity.

03

Request & Connect

Receivers submit a request with justification. Givers choose recipients.

04

Exchange & Review

Meet up or arrange delivery. Leave reviews to build community trust.
